- 2026-07-25 client: the ping-pong loop reads as a wobble, wants a true 360. Rebuilt as a real full rotation: 240 frames, lon 45 stepping 1.5 deg, so frame 239 sits exactly one step short of frame 0 and the loop closes with NO reverse concat. 16 fps = a 15 s rotation. mp4 1.9 MB (crf 36) / webm 2.3 MB (crf 50) / poster 374 KB, both verified with `ffmpeg -f null -` (zero decode errors) and 240 frames at 1600x900.
- While shooting the 360 the GDELT event layer turned out to be the real blocker: over CONUS its "ACTOR → ACTOR · event (Nx)" labels stack into a solid wall of grey text that hides the map. Kill it with `window.__registry.disable('conflict.gdelt.live')` (registry ids live in `apps/web/src/registry/defaults.ts`, `__registry` is a DEV global from App.tsx:152). Clicking the layer rail does NOT work here because the rail is collapsed at capture time. With it off you see thousands of individual aircraft glyphs over North America, which is the best frame in the loop. Leave the layer ON for the console/product shots; it is part of the product.
- Encode trap: two `encode-spin.sh` runs raced on the same output path and produced an mp4 full of "Invalid NAL unit size" errors that still probed as a valid 15 s file. Always re-run the encode alone and verify with `ffmpeg -v error -i out.mp4 -f null -` before trusting it.
- Hero contrast must be measured across the WHOLE loop, not one frame: pause the video at each second, hide the type, sample the brightest 2% of the backdrop per text box. Worst case across all 15 s is h1 3.18:1 (at t=0), sub 8.88:1, eyebrow 7.89:1, all passing. A single-frame reading is worthless here and once reported a bogus 12.94:1 because the video had not painted.
- Hero contrast was fixed from MEASURED pixels, not by eye: screenshot the hero with `.hero-inner > *` set to `visibility:hidden`, then sample the brightest 2% of the backdrop inside each text element's real bounding box. First pass measured h1 2.93:1 (needs 3.0) and eyebrow 1.91:1 (needs 4.5). A full-frame scrim fixes contrast by killing the image, so the fix is a local radial scrim under the type column only (`.hero-inner::before`, `right: 22%`) plus lifting the hero eyebrow to `#ccd6e0`. Final: h1 3.28:1, sub 8.80:1, eyebrow 8.15:1, all measured.
- Measurement trap: give the eyebrow `display:inline-block` before sampling. As a block it spanned 1160px, so the sample ran past the scrim into the bright globe and reported 1.86:1 for text that only occupies the first 343px.

An OPEN square is an archived fix, a SOLID square is the live one, and the stroke between them is the history you own, which is the product's actual differentiator.
