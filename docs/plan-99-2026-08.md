# The 99% — plan of record, 2026-08-05

The console rebuild (commits `026cbaf`..`53b76a7`) landed the *shell*. The
operator's verdict on the result, verbatim: *"it just looks not Palantir level
enough. It feels a bit cheap like a knockoff. There's insufficient data, and you
haven't analyzed it deep enough."* Plus a hard product requirement: **clean raw
data, not narrative** — no Telegram, no news aggregation, nothing that is
somebody's claim — processed locally, in parallel, because the good data is
gatekept behind paid APIs and has to be assembled from primary emitters instead.

This document is the plan for the remaining 99%. `docs/dashboard-redesign-2026-08.md`
remains the design authority for the shell; this one owns **what goes in it**.

---

## 0. The thesis

Every OSINT dashboard on the market fuses the same three commodity feeds
(ADS-B, AIS, news RSS) and calls it intelligence. Two of those are sensor data
and the third is narrative. Mixing them is the defect: a transponder cannot lie
about where it is, and a Telegram channel is *only* an assertion. Once they sit
in the same list the operator has no way to tell which is which, so the whole
surface inherits the credibility of its weakest source.

**Velocity's answer: every source carries a provenance tier, and the tier is
visible everywhere the data is.**

| Tier | Name | What it is | Can it lie? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T0** | `sensor` | A machine emitted it: transponders, AIS, seismometers, magnetometers, SAR, grid telemetry, GNSS, SDR | Only by malfunction or deliberate spoof, both of which are detectable and are themselves signal |
| **T1** | `registry` | An authority maintains it as a register of record: aircraft/vessel registries, company registers, sanctions lists, spectrum licences, orbital catalogues | Stale, rarely false |
| **T2** | `filing` | Somebody was legally compelled to disclose it: SEC/EDGAR, EU TED tenders, customs declarations, port state control, NOTAMs, procurement awards | Adversarially shaded, but on the record and dated |
| **T3** | `claim` | A human asserted it: news, social, Telegram, blogs | Freely |

**T3 is not deleted, it is demoted.** It never sources a fact, it never colours
the map, and it is off by default. Its only sanctioned use is *corroboration*:
given a T0/T1/T2 observation, does a claim exist that mentions it, and how long
after. That is a measurement about the claim, not a use of it.

This is a defensible product position and it is the answer to "why not just use
the other globe." Nobody else refuses to launder claims as facts.

## 2. Waves

Each wave ends with a commit, `pnpm -r typecheck` green, and its own check.

### W1 — the frame reads as an instrument — DONE (`328b025`)

1. **Globe fills its frame.** Default altitude drops from 20 Mm so the disk
   spans ~90% of the map viewport height, and the map viewport measures itself
   rather than assuming 60° over 1080px.
2. **The right dock is never empty.** With no selection it shows the **world
   summary**: live counts by tier, the freshest and stalest source, what changed
   in the last 15 minutes. Selecting an object replaces it.
3. **Layer rows tell the truth in four states**: `off` · `live n` · `0 · pulled 12s ago` · `—` (never fetched). No more identical grey bars.
4. **Toolbar groups get labels.**

*Check:* `node tools/perf/console_frame_check.mjs`. Measured 2026-08-05 at
1920x1080: disk fill **0.878** of the map frame at 11,651 km on a 1242x940
canvas, right dock rendering **653** characters with nothing selected, **27**
tier marks on visible rows, 0 console errors. The four row states are
deterministic and are guarded in `useLayerCounts.test.ts` instead, because which
state a row is in at any moment depends on what an upstream did in the last ten
seconds.

### W2 — provenance, everywhere — DONE (`328b025`)

1. `Tier = 'sensor' | 'registry' | 'filing' | 'claim'` on `LayerSpec`, on
   `Observation`, and on the API's source descriptors.
2. All 55+ layers tagged. A layer with no tier fails a guard.
3. Layers panel groups and filters by tier; `claim` is collapsed and off.
4. The entity dossier states the tier of every fact it shows.

*Check:* a guard asserting every registered layer carries a tier, plus a test
that `claim` layers are off in the default set.

### W3 — the raw-data expansion

The point of the tier work is that it creates room to add a lot of T0/T1/T2 and
stay honest. Target: **+25 keyless primary sources**, none of them narrative.
Full endpoint list and status in §3.

### W4 — local models, in parallel — DONE (`fa6d5bb`), measured 7.4x

Enrichment today is one-shot and synchronous per request. It becomes a **worker
pool over the llama.cpp sidecar**: a bounded queue, N slots sized to VRAM,
batched structured extraction, results written to the observation store and
served from cache. Nothing on the map waits on a model.

*Check:* a harness that submits M documents and reports throughput and p95, with
the pool size swept — a number, not an adjective.

### W5 — the analysis that is missing

Series (real, from `/api/history/tracks`), Histogram as a working cross-filter,
and correlation across tiers: which T0 observation and which T2 filing describe
the same object. This is where the product stops being a map.

## 2.1 Reachability, measured

`node tools/perf/app_reachability_check.mjs`, run 2026-08-05 against the live
backend. It drives every app through the store's DEV handle rather than the
launcher, so a broken launcher cannot hide a broken app behind it, and reports
rendered characters per surface.

yet ported in"* does not show up as a dead surface: `explorer` 22,145 ·
`country` 6,375 · `reports` 7,680 · `video` 3,553 · `investigate` 2,684 ·
`targeting` 2,486 · `sim` 2,315 · `graph` 2,343 · `map` 2,147 · `ai` 2,098 ·
`markets` 2,026 · `foundry` 1,837 · `city` 1,266 · `workflows` 1,119. Panels:
Info 893 · Layers 585 · Histogram 538 · Find 246.

Three requests fail, and two of them are **by design**, which the report is what
established:

| Request | Verdict |
|---|---|
| `401 /api/targets/board` | Not a defect. The board is local-first (`state/targetBoard.ts:271`, `if (!r.ok) return; // 401/503/502 → stay local`) and the server row is an optional sync. It is console noise, not a broken surface. |
| `401 /api/collab/shared-notes` | Same shape: Supabase-backed, absent on a keyless deployment. |
| `404 /api/ontology/object/control:workspace` | A real miss, and the only one. A workspace object is requested before anything has created it. |

`workflows` at 1,119 and `city` at 1,266 characters are the thinnest surfaces and
are where to look next for the operator's complaint.

## 4. Non-negotiables carried in

Everything in `apps/web/CLAUDE.md` and `apps/api/CLAUDE.md` holds unchanged. In
particular: no synthesised motion, `upsert` not `removeAll`, the guarded data
palette, one address per surface, no em dashes in dashboard copy, the lone `—`
means "no value reported", keyless is a product requirement, and the backend
test baseline never drops below what this branch inherited.
