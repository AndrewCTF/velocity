Accessibility floor

  • Every muted text tier clears 4.5:1 on both --bg-1 and --bg-2, in both themes, and the ramp stays monotonic. Parsed and asserted by theme/contrast.test.ts, which is the first thing any palette change has to clear.
  • Focus is always visible: 2px solid var(--accent) with a 2px offset.
  • Every interactive primitive is reachable and operable by keyboard, including the map toolbar and the link-analysis canvas, which are mouse only today.
  • Panels carry a role and a label. Rail resizers stay role="separator" with arrow, Home and End handling.
  • prefers-reduced-motion disables the selection pulse, turns camera slew into an instant set, and removes fades.
  • Nothing renders below 12px. The 780 sub-11px literals in the current app migrate onto --fs-cap, --fs-dense and --fs-body.
